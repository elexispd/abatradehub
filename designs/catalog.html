Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on sizes, quality checks, payments, and interstate parcel delivery.

swap_horiz

Can I mix different sizes and colours in one order?

Yes! As long as you meet the 10-piece minimum per style, you can freely mix sizes S to XXL and choose between our stock color options to suit your customer base.

verified

How do quality checks work before dispatch?

Our on-ground quality control team inspects seam allowances, zippers, button attachments, and clean iron pressing. We send you clear video and photo inspections on WhatsApp before waybilling.

local_shipping

How does delivery work across Nigeria & Africa?

We deliver via verified interstate motor parks (GIG, Peace, Young Shall Grow, God Is Good) for same/next day arrival in Nigeria, doorstep express couriers in Lagos/Abuja, and DHL/cargo for Ghana, Kenya, and Diaspora buyers.
