## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, dependable, and highly accessible B2B wholesale platform connecting African fashion manufacturers, tailors, and textile hubs with boutique owners, bulk merchants, and social commerce sellers across Lagos, Aba, Accra, Nairobi, and beyond.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Approachable & Trustworthy:** Commercial buying requires financial confidence. The experience replaces sterile enterprise interfaces and cluttered open-air marketplace feeds with an orderly, welcoming, and hospitable aesthetic.
- **Rooted & Authentic:** Visual choices honor African commerce—tactile fabrics, market hubs, rich soil, and linen surfaces—without resorting to tourist cliches or aloof high-fashion austerity.
- **Decisive & Transactional:** Wholesale buyers prioritize operational clarity: Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ), tiered bulk pricing, lead times, production capabilities, and instant verified communication via channels like WhatsApp.

### Design Movement
The style blends **Warm Modernist Commerce** with **Tactile Functionalism**. It avoids cold gray-scale tech defaults, opting instead for organic linen backgrounds, terracotta action anchors, and crisp structural borders that give retail merchants clear boundaries, low cognitive load, and absolute pricing legibility.

## Colors

The palette balances warm terracotta clay with organic grounding tones to inspire both commercial reliability and genuine regional familiarity.

### Functional Roles & Assignments
- **Primary (`#C05621` - Terracotta Clay):** Reserved for core high-intent actions—Request Bulk Quote, Place Order, Add to Sourcing Cart, and key transaction milestones.
- **Secondary (`#2D6A4F` - Deep Sage / Forest Green):** Represents credibility, supplier verification badges, factory audit tags, and secure payment / escrow states. It also naturally accommodates familiar WhatsApp quick-chat affordances (`#25D366` variant).
- **Tertiary (`#E0A96D` - Raw Ochre / Sisal):** Used for highlighting pricing tier intervals, production lead-time callouts, and secondary promotional chips.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#2A2521` - Deep Espresso):** Replaces pure digital black with a rich, readable dark tone for all structural typography, data tables, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Neutral Surface / Canvas (`#FDFBF7` & `#F7F4EE` - Linen & Warm Sand):** Base container surfaces that eliminate screen glare and communicate tactile textile warmth.
- **Subtle Borders (`#E8E3D9` - Thread Cream):** Outlines product cards, data grids, and filter groupings with soft contrast, avoiding clinical gray lines.

## Typography

The design system exclusively utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve cohesive geometric clarity with soft, friendly terminals. 

### Typographic Hierarchy & Principles
- **Clarity in Commerce:** Numbers, currency symbols (₦, $, GH₵, KSh), and MOQ quantities are set with medium-to-bold weights to avoid misinterpretation in outdoor light or fast mobile scanning.
- **Editorial Warmth:** Display sizes use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) and generous line heights to preserve breathing room, creating an organized, reputable vendor catalog rather than a chaotic discount clearinghouse.
- **Data Scannability:** Microcopy, MOQ tags, and supplier verification labels use `label-md` and `label-sm` with slight positive tracking for crisp readability on mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid anchors desktop views, folding into a 6-column layout on tablet, and a strict 4-column structure on mobile devices where a significant portion of African trade decisions occur.

### Adaptive Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width 1280px with centered margins (`2rem` / `32px`). Multi-column catalog displays show 3 to 4 wholesale product cards per row, alongside persistent filter drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 6 columns with `1.25rem` gutters. Category filters transition to a sticky horizontal scroll bar.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` outer canvas padding and `0.75rem` gutters. Wholesale catalog displays adapt to a balanced 2-column card grid, providing large touch targets (minimum 44px) for one-handed operation.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Spacing tokens govern element flow. Component groups (e.g., MOQ quantity alongside tier pricing) use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while sectional transitions use `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal Layering combined with Low-Contrast Warm Outlines**, avoiding synthetic drop shadows and stark grey barriers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Ground (Level 0):** Canvas base `#FDFBF7`. Provides the master canvas for browsing catalogs, directories, and market feeds.
- **Surface (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border of `#E8E3D9`. Used for product cards, supplier overview panels, and order status summaries. Provides distinct crispness without heavy casting.
- **Raised (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` with a subtle, warm-tinted ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(42, 37, 33, 0.06)`, bounded by a hairline border `#E8E3D9`. Applied to active hover states, sticky checkout summaries, and mobile bottom navigation bars.
- **Overlay (Level 3):** Modal sheets, bulk sample request forms, and WhatsApp quick-action popovers employ `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 37, 33, 0.12)` over a warm sand backdrop veil (`rgba(42, 37, 33, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 2`, elements standardize around a balanced **0.5rem (8px)** base radius. This creates a grounded, reliable commercial interface that avoids the sharpness of utilitarian software while staying away from bubbly, purely consumer styling.

### Shape Scale Applications
- **Base (8px / `0.5rem`):** Primary buttons, text inputs, wholesale product cards, filter containers, and notification panels.
- **Large (16px / `1rem`):** Vendor profile header banners, modal dialogs, and large featured garment collections.
- **Pill (Full Radius / `9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)** tags, verification chips, sourcing category badges, and quick-filter switches. The pill shape clearly separates metadata indicators from actionable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Bulk Action:** Solid `#C05621` background, white text (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` radius, bold label. Hover transforms to `#A84717`. Focus uses a 2px outline in `#C05621` with 2px offset.
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect:** Background `#2D6A4F` (or official `#25D366`), white text, featuring an inline brand icon. Directs buyers to direct factory communication.
- **Secondary / Request Sample:** `#F7F4EE` background with `#2A2521` text and a 1px `#E8E3D9` border. Provides clear utility without fighting primary transaction buttons.

### Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) & Category Chips
- **MOQ Badge:** Fully rounded pill container using a warm linen fill (`#F7F4EE`), 1px border (`#E8E3D9`), and espresso text (`#2A2521`). Displays structured text such as `MOQ: 20 pcs`.
- **Verified Factory Tag:** Pill container with `#2D6A4F` at 10% opacity, deep green text (`#2D6A4F`), accompanied by a shield or check icon for manufacturers with physically audited workshops.

### Product & Vendor Cards
- **Product Listing Card:** White `#FFFFFF` container with `0.5rem` radius, bordered by 1px `#E8E3D9`. Product photography features a natural linen backdrop (`#F7F4EE`). Content stacks: Category chip, garment title, tiered wholesale price (e.g., `₦4,500 / pc`), followed by the pill MOQ label.
- **Vendor Trust Banner:** Displays manufacturer location (e.g., *Ariaria Market, Aba* or *Lekki, Lagos*), years active, monthly output capacity, and quick-quote triggers.

### Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Clean 44px min-height fields on `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E8E3D9` borders and `0.5rem` corner radius. Focused inputs shift to a 1.5px border of `#C05621`.
- **Bulk Tier Stepper:** Specialized input combining numerical entry with tiered bulk discount notices (e.g., *50–199 pcs: ₦4,500* | *200+ pcs: ₦3,800*).

### Steppers & Order Progress Indicators
- Linear progress tracker using connected nodes. Active phases feature solid terracotta dots (`#C05621`), completed stages utilize deep forest green (`#2D6A4F`) checks, and pending stages remain warm muted sand (`#E8E3D9`).
